# Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis – Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis is a Power BI project focused on analysing business sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, and market trends.

This project uses interactive dashboards and data visualization to understand business performance, identify profitable products, evaluate discount strategies, and generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objective
To analyse sales and profit performance across different regions, markets, product categories, and customer segments to identify business growth opportunities, understand profitability, and support better business decisions.

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Sales and Profit Overview
- Analysed total sales, profit, quantity sold, and order performance.
- Compared sales and profit across different years and regions.
- Identified top-performing and low-performing markets.
- Evaluated overall business performance and profitability.

### 2. Product, Category & Discount Analysis
- Analysed sales and profit across different product categories and sub-categories.
- Identified top-selling and low-performing products.
- Examined the impact of discounts on sales and profitability.
- Identified products with high sales but low profit.

### 3. Customer, Segment & Market Analysis
- Analysed customer performance across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Compared sales and profitability across different markets and regions.
- Identified top-performing customers based on sales and profit.
- Evaluated customer purchasing patterns and market performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 📈 Key Insights
- Analysed overall sales of approximately 13M and total profit of approximately 1.47M.
- Identified top-performing regions based on sales and profitability.
- Evaluated the performance of different product categories and sub-categories.
- Identified the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Compared business performance across customer segments and markets.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing products and profitable categories.
- Optimize discount strategies to improve profit margins.
- Improve the performance of low-profit products.
- Strengthen customer relationships across different segments.
- Focus on business expansion in high-potential markets.
- Monitor sales and profit trends to support better decisions.
